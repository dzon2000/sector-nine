## Tentative 12×12 recovery path (not yet demonstrated)

Start from an exported genuine v0.3 DONE save, preserve all positions and inventory. Keep one profitable old gear/steel/coal flow to earn research and construction cash. Reveal candidate deposit A/B tiles. If occupied, player *explicitly* removes/moves the obstructing structure, retaining standard 75% demolition refund; never displace it automatically. Construct one copper extractor + silicon extractor and physical terminal routes; sell 10 each for M8. Purchase Circuit Fabrication and Industrial Power, position generator fed from a coal mine, route 2:1 resources to fabricator and output to terminal; prove M9. For M10 expand fabrication and power while preserving five steel sales per 60 ticks. This is a staged **strategy hypothesis**, not a tested layout or guaranteed recoverable route. WP3 must derive coordinates and show actual command-by-command capital floor, occupancy, arbitration, profit and mistakes from preserved save.

## WP1 entry test list (write failing tests before mechanics)

1. Typed, deposited copper/silicon only; physical intake and audited conversion (2:1).
2. Single generator no same-tick power from incoming coal; exact expiry at 1 and 12 ticks.
3. Two persistent fabricator demands alternate under one available unit; array-order independence and saved round-robin cursor.
4. Underpower freezes processing and preserves reserved stock; output backpressure cannot duplicate units or money.
5. Coal burned as new auditable consumed inventory and no phantom sale; power deficit counters correctly separate simulation demand from player-labeled waiting.
6. Existing ingot/coal/gear/steel benchmark fixtures remain unchanged; save/load deterministic.
