**Status** — Developer evidence for AC5. Numeric rules proposed in the [draft ADR](../decisions/proposed-v0.7-isolation-log.md). Owner freeze and independent QA pending.
**Source baseline** — `9ac69db455f608c01d6e7cb625e8b47e0bccd1af`. Probe files live under `tests/integration/economy`. The shipped engine, gameplay and save rules are unchanged.

## Reproduce

```sh
npm ci
npm test -- tests/integration/economy/v0.7-isolation-probe.test.ts --maxWorkers=1
```

`V07-WP4-CANDIDATES` and `V07-WP4-RECOVERY` hold exact per-block trace arrays in this order: gears made, gears sold, circuits made, circuits sold, max circuit-sale drought. Money fields are in hundredths of a credit. A `null` completion is failure to qualify within the trace. Each physical interval uses the ordinary engine and terminal. The probe uses material-ledger production deltas and terminal diagnostics cross-checked against the sales ledger. It asserts at most one terminal sale per tick, balances against recorded revenue/cost, and reconciles every material against physical factory stock. Current-save JSON round-trips and next-tick state equality are checked.

## Deliberate faults, recovery and cadence

Each test starts with the proposed higher-gear factory, then breaks one branch with a normal command. Run 120 active ticks to settle existing conveyor and machine cargo, then inspect six full blocks (180 ticks) while the fault stays in place. The unaffected product still meets its goals. Restore the branch and continue the same measurement timeline, including carried gaps and the reset qualifying streak. Repair takes one command with zero construction spend.

| Deliberate fault | Full drained blocks that pass, all layouts | What still holds | First pass after repair I / C / mixed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Point the gear branch conveyor (1,7) east into the vacant tile or mixed bypass | 0/6 | 7–8 circuits made and sold, gap 3–4 | 60 / 60 / 60 ticks |
| Favor raw copper shipments at splitter (7,1) using forward priority | 0/6 | 10–12 gears made and sold | 90 / 90 / 90 ticks |

Under the gear fault, measured gear production/sales fell to 6–8 per block across the three layouts. The mixed bypass creates backpressure when the rotated (1,7) conveyor points back into it. Under the copper fault, fewer circuits reach the terminal and the sale drought reaches at least seven ticks. Recovery returns (1,7) to north or sets (7,1) to clockwise. See the exact JSON trace for cash floors and first recovery blocks.

The test drives the existing simulation clock at 1× and 2× with controlled timers and the same off-boundary, tick-indexed gear fault and repair. Final factory/save state and all measured evidence are identical after 180 ticks. Active clock time is 180 s versus 90 s. Stopping the clock contributes no samples. Boundary fixtures exercise partial blocks, a sale at the start of a block ending its predecessor's drought, and a dry run extending across adjacent blocks. Sales-only backlogs and production-only flows cannot qualify.

For the two paid references, M16 needs **30 s at 1× or 15 s at 2×**. After an immediate M17 edit, the measured high wait is **90 s at 1× or 45 s at 2×** for both routes. Total active wait is **120 s / 60 s** with an immediate successful edit, or **90 s / 45 s** when M17 starts with the prepared cadence. These are operating durations, excluding time spent inspecting, planning, editing or pausing. An edit made partway through a block can add waiting for a full valid block. No maximum playtime applies to a stalled layout.

## Limits and handoff

Feasibility is based on two paid campaign replays and a command-built branch variant. This is no evidence of player discovery, real-time playtest pacing, alternate-build visual guidance or production Chapter V save compatibility. WP5 needs explicit approval for implementing the proposed rules and validating natural progression, direct Chapter V starts, archive, console and current save behavior. Human play remains a later acceptance step.
