## Implemented headless design

- New provisional item types: `copper_ore`, `silicon`, `circuit`. Copper extractor and silicon extractor are fixed-deposit, directionally outputting machines. Candidate new deposits `(8,2)` copper and `(8,9)` silicon are explicitly **not frozen**.
- Circuit fabricator reserves exactly two copper ore and one silicon for one circuit, then requires six granted power ticks per processing cycle. Processing does not advance under shortage. Output completion/cost and material consumption occur exactly once; blocked output never creates duplicate material.
- Generator accepts physically transferred coal into typed buffer. Charging fuel occurs after transfer and produces no power until the next active tick. Each charged coal is booked as `burned` once, distinct from `consumed`, `sold` and `demolished`; the stock conservation identity includes that burned quantity. Fuel is used for each active supply tick even with no consumers, with no implicit energy storage.
- Abstract site-wide capacity aggregates fueled generators. Circuit fabricators alone request power; all old machines stay independent of power. Stable numeric-ID round-robin, saved `lastServedFabricatorId`, and sorted active demanders prevent accidental dependence on entity iteration order.
- Tick snapshots include `supply`, `demand`, `served`, `demandCovered`, `unpoweredProcessingTicks`, `coalBurned`, fuel counter and bounded 60-tick power history. Zero demand yields `demandCovered=false`. Save validation rejects negative/over-limit fuel, malformed allocation/cursor, bad history and missing power state when power buildings exist.
- The old `createGame()` state does not acquire a power object without new powered buildings; the first generator/fabricator placement creates one. This maintains the shape of old mission fixtures and avoids rewriting historical power state. Older save item counts and ledger values remain intact with only additive zeros for new item keys.

## Regression and accounting guardrails

Prior approved reference profits must remain **58.50 Cr/60 ticks ingots; 38.00 Cr coal; 92.25 Cr gears; 116.625 Cr steel**. The unchanged historical integration fixtures remain authoritative, not the isolated new numeric hypotheses. New-model capital/economy and the authenticity of v0.3 DONE continuation are **not** proved by artificial 1,000,000-cent WP1 isolation fixtures.

WP1 uses provisional values only: copper mine 7,500 cents, silicon extractor 8,500, circuit fabricator 16,000, generator 11,000; rates 3/4 extraction ticks, 6 circuit powered ticks, 12 generator ticks/coal, 1 supply unit/generator, and the candidate new item costs/sale prices in [WP0 report](v0.4-wp0-baseline.md). These are **experiment parameters**, not final design decisions.
