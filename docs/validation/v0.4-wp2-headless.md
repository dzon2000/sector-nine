## 4. W2.2–W2.4 — Mission completion and anti-stockpile

- **M8 Unregistered Materials**: after explicit M8 activation, track real terminal sales of 10 copper and 10 silicon in mission-local cumulative counters; Surveying must be owned. Old lifetime counters and production stock do not complete it.
- **M9 A Circuit in the Dark**: after new M9 activation, require paid Fabrication + Industrial Power, and four circuits physically sold; the physical engine's power/fuel/material production remains authoritative.
- **M10 Signal Integrity**: for each *complete* trailing 60 active-tick window since M10 activation, require `circuit sales >= 11`, `circuit physical completions >= 11`, `steel sales >= 5`, and `covered nonzero demand ticks >= 57`; retain the combined requirement for **60 consecutive evaluated windows**. A failing full window resets the streak. The created `ChapterTick` captures actual terminal event totals, actual `materialLedger.circuit.produced` delta and actual `power.demandCovered`. Pauses, offline time, stock before activation and zero-demand ticks cannot increment progress.
- Tests explicitly cover incomplete 59/60 windows, 10/11 circuits sold, 10/11 produced, 4/5 steel, 56/57 coverage, exact 59/60 streak, reset, recovery and corrupted history rejection.
- The WP3 four-staged-circuit + one-real-fabricator exploit qualifies for 63 windows under old sales-only rules but **zero** under this owner-approved new production guard; the legitimate two-fabricator factory qualifies for 241 under the stricter condition.

## 5. W2.5–W2.8 — Chapter state and version-on-activation migration

- A real v0.3 `DONE` is preserved as the foundry milestone. Only an explicit `beginChapterII(state)` command enters M8; doing nothing leaves the old `DONE` sandbox unchanged.
- The command requires `DONE` and the established Chapter I research proof, preserves all entity positions, materials, money, old sales and progress, and initializes `chapterII.foundryCompleted/started/completed/storySeen`, plus fresh M8 metrics. It sets **schemaVersion 4** and **missionRuleVersion 4** at the explicit Chapter II transition.
- Pre-activation legacy v1/v2 migrations and v3 M5/M6/M7/DONE loads retain their existing schema-3/mission-rule-2-or-3 semantics until explicit activation. This avoids modifying or resetting ongoing 120-tick M6/M7 streaks or retroactively entering a new chapter.
- Schema v4 snapshots contain exact fixed copper and silicon deposit metadata, six typed technology flags, optional saved power/fuel arbitration, the durable Chapter I achievement, bounded new Chapter II sale/production/coverage windows, mission activation epoch, evaluated tick and streak, and story visibility flag. V4 `DONE` requires `chapterII.completed=true` with no stale active metrics.
- `validateSave` rejects malformed/foreign deposits, missing research prerequisites for M9/M10, bad flags, incomplete M10 histories, invalid window chronology, negative counters, invalid timestamps, stale data, missing power state and invalid splitter cursor. It operates on a cloned save before acceptance. A failed load must not overwrite stored data.
- Existing v0.3 occupied copper tile (8,2) remains occupied after Chapter II activation; metadata revelation never displaces or demolishes it.

**Migration scope clarification:** New Chapter II state is deliberately migrated to schema 4 **on explicit activation**, not on loading a legacy in-progress chapter. This is a versioned save transition, not a mandatory rewrite of every old save merely because v0.4 code runs.
