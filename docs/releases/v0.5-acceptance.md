## Owner playtest

1. Start recording in **Inspector → Local playtest** before Chapter III. Review both M12 previews, pick one route, and complete M11–M13 with your own factory decisions.
2. Tag planning, credit waiting and mission/stability waiting with the existing controls. Note unclear objectives, repeated work, route consequences and the factory changes needed for M13.
3. During an active contract, pause, wait for **Saved**, reload, and check the route, active objective and streak. In a disposable fresh campaign or separate browser profile, exercise cancellation and confirmed restart without losing the playtest save.
4. Stop and export the JSON report. Share it with your notes, including which restore and reset checks you exercised, and confirm whether the chapter is ready for release. A focused playtest of the second route can follow.

The owner evidence and release decisions will be recorded on [PR #11](https://github.com/dzon2000/sector-nine/pull/11).
