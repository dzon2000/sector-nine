## 1. Goal and scope recommendation

Turn the successful electronics/power optimization loop into a bounded Chapter III investigation. Add contracts and one meaningful choice using existing products and machines. Remove legacy-save overhead in a separate package first.

Recommend a small authored campaign over a generic repeatable-contract marketplace. It provides a concrete next goal after M10 while avoiding procedural generation, new production chains and economy-wide reward tuning in the same release.

Approved scope:

- WP1: one current-version save format; remove legacy migrations and obsolete rule branches.
- Chapter III, **Project Prometheus**, with three authored steps M11–M13 after M10 and explicit player activation. Free play remains available before activation and after completion.
- One active contract at a time; fixed authored offers, activation-local objectives and exactly-once completion.
- One route choice: investigate Prometheus autonomously or cooperate with the corporate archive team. Change the next production objective and narrative response, then reconverge on M13. No permanent technology lockout, second currency or duplicate campaign tree.
- Non-cash rewards: recovered archive entries and next-mission access. Existing terminal sales still earn normal revenue. Cash bonuses, price multipliers and discounts are deferred, avoiding hidden subsidies and unapproved economics.
- Clear mission/contract UI and recorder evidence for selections, transitions and waiting categories.

Not in scope: new buildings/resources/recipes, robots, expanded maps, warehouses, offline progression, electrical networks, procedural/repeatable contracts, real-time deadlines, penalties, legacy save conversion or economy retuning. M4 60 Cr / M7 190 Cr suggestions remain unapproved and outside this proposal. Existing M1–M10 rules, prices, map coordinates and 12×12 geography remain fixed.

## 2. Proposed mission arc

WP2 verified the following contracts against a paid 300 Cr campaign. See the [WP2 evidence](../validation/v0.5-wp2.md) and [ADR-0007](../decisions/0007-prometheus-contracts.md).

| Step | Narrative / decision | Factory challenge | Completion intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M11 — Recover the Archive | Trace the first signal to a damaged industrial archive | Sustain circuits and gears together, forcing a trade-off against the preceding circuit/steel layout | Full post-activation sale and physical-production windows; sustained qualifying streak |
| M12 — Choose the Recipient | Choose independent investigation or corporate cooperation before objective activation | Independent route emphasizes powered circuit continuity; corporate route emphasizes mixed metallurgy/electronics delivery | Two distinct, feasible profiles; same progression access; no permanent economic advantage |
| M13 — Reconstruct Prometheus | Reconstruct a failed interlock identified in the recovered archive | Make circuits for its controller, gears for its mechanism, and steel for its housing | Combined mission-local production/sales and profit conditions; Chapter III completion |

Use a trailing window of 60 active ticks and require 30 consecutive qualifying full windows. Each product quota requires both physical production and terminal sales in the same window.

| Contract | Minimum production and sales of each item per window | Additional condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M11 | 12 circuits, 6 gears | — |
| M12 Independent | 9 circuits | All 60 ticks have positive, fully covered fabrication demand. At most 5 coal units burned by generators. |
| M12 Corporate | 12 circuits, 10 steel | — |
| M13 | 13 circuits, 12 gears, 7 steel | At least 600.00 Cr operating profit. |

M13 rewards adapting the product mix. A representative corporate factory can convert its second steel branch into a gear line. A representative independent factory can add a gear branch and restore circuit capacity. Factory edits are not contract conditions. A prepared layout can qualify with sufficient new output.

## 3. Approved contract semantics

- Preconditions: M10 complete, Chapter III explicitly started, previous step completed. The offer preview shows products, measurements, rewards and consequences before confirmation.
- Acceptance establishes the objective's activation tick. Prior sales/production and pre-choice windows cannot qualify. Full windows are mandatory before streak evaluation; failing evaluated windows reset streaks. Paused, hidden-tab and offline time never advance objectives.
- Objectives observe physical terminal sales and authoritative production events. Sales consume goods once through the existing terminal, at unchanged prices. A contract does not consume/sell the same item again or add virtual deliveries.
- For every production target, require newly produced goods as well as sales so stockpiles alone cannot complete it. Each full window evaluates every approved condition together.
- Contract/reward transitions occur once. Repeated commands, replayed UI events and reload at the completion boundary cannot repeat rewards, sales or unlocks.
- M12 choice becomes final only after explicit confirmation. It persists through reload. Both routes reconverge; neither locks out core technologies or makes campaign completion depend on a discarded alternative.
- No deadlines or failure penalties. A struggling factory can rebuild and recover using normal sales. Narrative can be skipped/replayed without changing mission state or granting rewards again.
- Operating profit remains sales revenue minus operating costs, excluding capital/refunds/research. No new economic category is needed for narrative rewards.

## 4. Persistence and regression boundaries

Apply ADR-0006 throughout the campaign, not only Chapter III. New-game state has the current schema before M1; Chapter II/III transitions never masquerade as migrations. Distinguish unsupported version from corrupt current-version data. Loading errors halt gameplay/autosave until a safe user decision; explicit restart initializes a valid current-version campaign.

Current save identity must be independent of build commit. Preserve honest recorder build/schema/rule provenance, opt-in local collection, privacy and no fabricated timing intent. Reader compatibility for old playtest report formats is separate from game-save compatibility; retain it unless an explicit scope decision removes it.

Remove legacy-only migration/mission paths, not current Chapter I/II mechanics, stock/money validation, serialized writes, deterministic fairness or anti-stockpile checks. Preserve direct placement/rotation and occupied-deposit UI behavior from v0.4.
