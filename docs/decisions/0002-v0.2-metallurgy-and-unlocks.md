# ADR-0002 — v0.2 Advanced Metallurgy and Technology Unlocks

**Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Status:** accepted  
**Specification:** [SEKTOR 9 v0.2](../specifications/v0.2.md)

## Context

v0.1 demonstrated extraction, smelting, physical transportation, missions, and deterministic accounting. v0.2 must introduce meaningful production choices without broadening to power, offline progression, or research laboratories.

## Decision

- Introduce coal mining, mechanical gears, and steel with generic, typed, multi-input recipes.
- Use **achievement and credit payments** for technology unlocks. No research point currency, labs, or research timers in v0.2.
- Keep the **12 × 12 grid**. Add coal deposits at **(7,9)** and **(5,9)** without relocating any existing structures on migrated saves.
- For **new games only**, gate basic furnace construction until M2 completes; grandfather existing v0.1 structures and prior milestones.
- Research costs: **80 Cr** coal extraction, **120 Cr** mechanical components, **180 Cr** advanced smelting, total **380 Cr**. Unlocks must be manually confirmed and remain permanent.
- Add narrative continuation and missions **M5–M7** after M4, without prematurely starting Chapter II.
- Preserve v0.1 economic regression, transport invariants, and save state through schema v1 → v2 migration.
- Automate scenario economics and report throughput, profit, conservation, and incremental payback in CI.

## Validation boundary

The exact prices and pacing in the specification are an **approved initial balancing hypothesis**, not measured outcomes. The headless engine must validate and report deviations. Proposed adjustments require an explicit, reviewed specification/ADR update. The target M4→M7 journey of 20–40 minutes is a playtest goal.

## Consequences

Implementation requires typed buffer/recipe refactoring, compatibility tests for v0.1 saves, a technology progression module, and new economy fixtures. All work belongs to the separate v0.2 implementation chat and feature branch.
