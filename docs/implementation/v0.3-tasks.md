## 1. Frozen product requirements

| Mission | Frozen condition | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| M5 | Sell 10 coal | Physical terminal sales; unchanged |
| M6 | >= 9 gears in **each trailing 60 active ticks** for **120 consecutive active ticks** | Fresh mission-local measurement window required |
| M7 | >= 7 gears AND >= 7 steel AND >= 175.00 Cr **operating profit**, each in **the trailing 60 active ticks**, for **120 consecutive active ticks**; Advanced Smelting unlocked | Simultaneous requirements; no capital/research in profit |

The 60-tick window starts **after mission activation**. No pre-activation sales or profit should leak into mission measurements. The streak begins only with a complete mission-local 60-tick window and resets to zero after any failing evaluated tick. Pause/hidden-tab inactivity does not advance simulation. Completion is permanent. The engine counts actual physical sales, not produced stock.

**Compatibility decision:** Legacy v0.2 saves in active M6 or M7 continue against the original sell-8-gears / sell-8-steel rules. An active M5 save retains M5, then enters v0.3 M6. `DONE` remains complete. New campaigns follow v0.3 rules. Preserve existing purchase/unlock state, cash, stock, transports, counters and achievements during schema migration.
