# SEKTOR 9 v0.2 — Acceptance Review

**Version:** v0.2 Advanced Metallurgy & Technology Unlocks  
**Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Source:** `docs/specifications/v0.2.md`, ADR-0002  
**Base:** `develop`  
**Feature branch:** `feature/v0.2-advanced-metallurgy`  
**Review state:** Ready for GitHub PR review; **not released or merged**.

## Validation approach

- Headless Vitest suites cover deterministic simulation, typed recipes, terminal sale accounting, purchases, mission transitions, conservation and save migration.
- Exact v0.1 reference economy fixture is unchanged: **30 ore produced / 15 ingots sold / 58.50 Cr operating profit** over a 60-tick steady-state window.
- Gear and steel scenarios use a 600-tick warm-up and 240-tick measurement window.
- Browser Playwright suites cover first-run narrative, new foundry directive, technology purchase and persistence, coal construction and keyboard/pause/reset compatibility.
- A full headless campaign executes **actual commands only** (place, demolish, purchase, tick, save/restore) starting with **300.00 Cr**. No invented income, debug state, price adjustment or forced milestone is used.
- User confirmed manual playability and working UI/story/missions up through WP5 on 2026-11-08.

## WP6 full campaign evidence

**CI baseline:** [Actions #42522182990](https://github.com/dzon2000/sector-nine/actions/runs/42522182990) on `aac110e` — success (build, 66 Vitest cases, 8 Playwright cases).

| Stage | Tick from new game | Cash at checkpoint |
|---|---:|---:|
| M3 (first ingot mission) | 26 | 232.50 Cr |
| M5 (foundry interlude) | 173 | 292.95 Cr |
| M6 (coal sales complete) | 450 | 418.40 Cr |
| M7 (gear sales complete) | 531 | 220.95 Cr |
| DONE (steel deliveries complete) | 844 | 848.00 Cr |

Cumulative financial reconciliation: starting capital **300.00 Cr**, construction **715.00 Cr**, demolition refunds **11.25 Cr**, research fees **380.00 Cr**, terminal receipts **2,865.00 Cr**, operating cost **1,233.25 Cr**, ending cash **848.00 Cr**. All figures integer cents; reconciliation verified by a headless assertion.

Lifetime sales at completion: 12 ore, 185 ingots, 117 coal, 47 gears, 8 steel. A small misplaced belt was deliberately demolished; the player recovered without debug or additional funds. At M7, the scenario continued producing profitable goods after purchasing the technology until sufficient cash was available for the advanced furnace. All three technologies are permanently unlocked.

**Pacing observation:** 844 active ticks (approximately 14 minutes of uninterrupted simulation) for one optimized end-to-end reference script, with M5→DONE taking 671 ticks (approximately 11 minutes). The specification's experienced-player pacing target of 20–40 minutes is a playtest target rather than a simulation invariant. Human decision time and construction interaction are excluded; no balance constants have been changed. A separate design review is required before retuning pacing.
