## 1. Decision summary

**Working title:** *First Signal*. Chapter I's foundry is restored. Chapter II opens with an anonymous electronics procurement request and an anomalous signature linked to Project Prometheus.

v0.4 must deliver four outcomes:

1. Make existing factories easier to operate through direct pointer orientation and a stable map/mission viewport.
2. Make playtest exports reproducible through rules/schema/build provenance and more precise player-declared waiting categories.
3. Introduce a compact electronics chain using two new physical resources and one powered, two-input processing recipe.
4. Add a deliberately bounded power challenge and sustained mission objectives that demand reconfiguration rather than passive waiting.

The roadmap already assigns **electronics and energy** to v0.4. Avoid expanding the release into a full power-grid topology, wire intermediate products, storage, new maps, offline production, contracts, or randomized failures.

## 3. UX requirements — issues #5 and #7

### 3.1 Direct rotation gesture (#5)

- In selection mode, pressing an existing rotatable entity and dragging in a cardinal direction previews its facing; release commits the direction **once**, directly, independent of its original facing. Determine cardinal direction from the dominant press-to-release vector: right E, left W, up N, down S.
- A short or stationary click continues to select/inspect. A movement threshold prevents incidental rotation. The initially pressed entity is the only possible target.
- Escape, pointer cancellation, lost capture, or invalid release cancels without mutation. Releasing on an adjacent tile must not select or rotate that other tile.
- The sales terminal remains nonrotatable. Placement and demolition are not silently converted into rotation gestures.
- Keep keyboard **R** and the explicit Rotate button. Preserve keyboard access and scaled-canvas coordinate correctness at browser zoom and CSS scaling.
- One successful direction change = one successful rotation command in the opt-in recorder; choosing the existing direction is a no-op, with no rotation event.
- Cover N/E/S/W, short drag, cancel, adjacent target, scaled board, save/reload and command counts using Playwright pointer tests.

### 3.2 Fixed map and mission viewport (#7)

- On **all supported desktop viewport sizes**, the header, the entire centered 12×12 map, and the active mission summary/progress must remain visible at the same time **without page-level scrolling**. This is a continuous responsive-layout requirement, not a rule limited to selected resolutions. Verify at minimum **1280×720**, **1366×768**, and **1920×1080**, including long sidebar content.
- Independently scroll construction/technology panel on the left and inspector/diagnostics/recorder panel on the right, including at **1920×1080** and higher resolutions. A long technology tree or story detail must not expand document height or push the board or mission HUD off screen.
- Scale the square map down when space is constrained, preserving aspect ratio, clear pointer-to-tile mapping and readable feedback; never clip the board.
- The mission HUD is persistent in a bounded bottom region. A long narrative/objective scrolls locally; the goal metrics remain glanceable.
- On narrow/short screens, prioritize board + mission HUD and expose tool/inspector content through accessible collapsible panels or a tabbed surface; do not force an unworkable three-column layout.
- E2E assertions must inspect bounding rectangles, page scroll/overflow, independent sidebar scroll, scaled hit targets, focus order, keyboard control and dialogs.

## 4. Recorder reproducibility and observation quality — issue #6 plus playtest feedback

- Preserve existing `schemaVersion` and `missionRuleVersion` values captured from the **actual save at recording start**; do not substitute the package's stale `0.1.0` version.
- Embed the precise build-time Git commit SHA into the Vite bundle and export as `buildCommit`. CI must supply the commit that generated the frontend artifact, **not** the current branch tip or runtime GitHub state. For a local build with no reliable metadata use `null` (documented), not a made-up hash. Optional `releaseLabel` is informational only.
- Preserve provenance across reload; a recording started before an app upgrade retains its originating SHA. If a recording spans builds, either record build transitions or explicitly label mixed-build results as limited. Old sessions and exports missing new fields must still load without invented values.
- Keep recording opt-in, local-only, without identifiers, server calls or clock-dependent simulation behavior.
- Add a distinct player-declared **Waiting for mission/stability** activity category separate from **Waiting for credits**, **Planning/routing**, **Paused**, and **Unclassified**. Existing reports remain valid. Do not infer intent from inactivity or retroactively change old owner data.
- Export should include observed power-supply deficits for v0.4, keeping simulation-derived counters separate from human-labeled wall time.

## 5. New gameplay — constrained Chapter II electronics and energy

### 5.1 Resources and buildings

**Proposed new materials:** `copper_ore`, `silicon`, `circuit`. Existing iron, coal, ingots, gears and steel remain unchanged.

**Proposed new structures:**

| Structure | Function | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Copper Mine | Extracts copper ore from a fixed copper deposit | Physical directional output |
| Silicon Extractor | Extracts silicon from a fixed silicon deposit | Physical directional output |
| Circuit Fabricator | Consumes two copper ore + one silicon; produces one circuit | Requires electricity during processing |
| Coal Generator | Receives physical coal and supplies abstract site-wide power capacity | Fuel and output determined by deterministic ticks |

This is intentionally a **minimal** electronics chain. Separate copper wire, silicon wafers, circuit tiers, power poles/cables, grid topology and battery/storage components are deferred. The long-term GDD mentions wire; v0.4 deliberately starts with a two-input circuit recipe to avoid introducing a new recipe-selection system in the same release.

**Map constraint:** retain the 12×12 board and fixed terminal. Add copper/silicon deposits at fixed, documented coordinates selected through layout feasibility testing. Do not overwrite, move, or destroy legacy entities during upgrade. If a newly revealed deposit is occupied by an old structure, the old structure stays until the player explicitly removes it. The campaign must have a recoverable route to the required mine placement. Do not silently expand the map to make fixtures pass.

**Physical simulation:** copper, silicon, circuits and generator coal travel on the existing directional conveyor system with current capacity/backpressure/arbitration semantics. Circuit sales occur only upon actual terminal intake. Existing item/accounting invariants apply to the new materials.

### 5.2 Minimal power model

Recommended v0.4 power design:

- A coal generator accepts physical coal through its input buffer. One fuel unit powers a fixed, deterministic number of future active ticks (`fuelTicksPerCoal`: numerical proposal to validate). No energy item is transported on conveyors.
- A fuelled generator contributes a fixed number of power units per tick. A circuit fabricator needs one unit on each tick when its production cycle requires processing. Insufficient power **stalls** processing, without losing reserved inputs, duplicating output, or advancing the fabrication cycle.
- Existing iron/coal mines, furnaces, gear assemblers, advanced furnaces and conveyors remain outside v0.4's power budget. This avoids retroactive failure of old factories and eliminates coal-generator bootstrap deadlocks. Later releases may unify power requirements in a separate ADR.
- Site-wide virtual distribution; no cables, adjacency radius, loss, blackouts or weather. Multiple generators aggregate supply. Define deterministic allocation when demand exceeds supply, using a saved fair arbiter or another explicitly validated stable policy; do not make allocation depend accidentally on React rendering, entity iteration order or wall time.
- Define fuel intake vs power-production tick ordering explicitly so same-tick transfers cannot produce ambiguous free energy. Save fuel counters and allocation state. Expose `supply`, `demand`, `unpowered processing ticks`, and fuel remaining in diagnostics.

**Design invariants:** existing recipes/profit fixtures stay identical; power-related operating costs and new equipment prices apply only to new objects; no automatic changes to old prices.

### 5.3 Research progression

New research gates (names proposed, costs TBD):

1. **Resource Surveying** — requires Chapter I foundry completion; reveals copper/silicon deposits and their extractors.
2. **Circuit Fabrication** — requires verified copper/silicon delivery, unlocks Circuit Fabricator.
3. **Industrial Power** — unlocks Coal Generator; must be available before first circuit production. The UI teaches why a fabricator will not run without fuelled power.

The order between the second and third unlocks may remain flexible, but the narrative/tutorial must describe both prerequisites. Prices, extraction frequencies, circuit cycle/cost/sale price and generator fuel duration are **unapproved** until the physical benchmark and capital-recovery test pass.

## 6. Proposed missions M8–M10

The numbers below are **specific design targets to test, not approved balance constants**. A playtest and real-engine construction/economy fixture must establish feasibility on the unchanged 12×12 map.

| ID | Title | Proposed completion rule | Design lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M8** | **Unregistered Materials** | Unlock Resource Surveying; physically sell **10 copper ore and 10 silicon** through the existing terminal after M8 starts. | Discover and route two new resource streams while preserving income. |
| **M9** | **A Circuit in the Dark** | Unlock Circuit Fabrication and Industrial Power; physically sell **4 circuits** after M9 activation, using real copper, silicon and fuel-derived electricity. | Assemble a two-input product and diagnose fuel/power starvation. |
| **M10** | **Signal Integrity** | In each complete trailing **60 active ticks** after M10 activation, sell **at least 11 circuits** and **5 steel**, and supply all active electronic processing demand on **at least 57 of those 60 ticks**. Maintain the combined condition for **60 consecutive qualifying active ticks**. | Reorganize legacy and electronics production, add fabrication/power capacity and preserve a parallel steel line. |

**Why M10 = 11 circuits?** With the provisional one-circuit-per-six-processing-ticks recipe, a lone continuously powered fabricator cannot exceed 10 circuits/60 ticks after warm-up. Eleven requires more than one fabricator and, with one unit of generation serving one active fabricator, forces consideration of additional power supply. **This argument is only a theoretical upper bound; physical conveyor and generator fixtures must verify the target.**

M10 power-availability numerator is a tick-level boolean: *all circuit fabricators requesting power for processing that tick were served*. A tick with no electronic power demand must not count as evidence of a well-powered plant. Require a meaningful demand during qualification and make the denominator/empty-demand treatment explicit in implementation tests. Completed sales alone do not waive the power condition.

**Shared mission semantics:** use mission-local post-activation terminal-sale and power histories; require a fully populated 60-tick window; failed ticks reset the 60-tick streak; no offline progress; pause/reload preserves exact streak and buffered items. Old cumulative data cannot complete new gates. The active M10 HUD must display circuits, steel, powered-tick coverage, and stability streak concurrently.

**Fallback if tests show M10 is infeasible:** retune **new** mission thresholds or new component parameters through an explicit recorded design decision; do not change existing iron/coal/gear/steel economics and do not add hidden resources or debug credit injections.

## 7. Chapter II — The Unknown Client (story design)

### Narrative opening (after completing v0.3's foundry)

> **UNREGISTERED PROCUREMENT REQUEST // ROUTE 09**  
> Sender: UNKNOWN  
> “The foundry is online. Steel was the key, not the destination. We need conductors and silicon. Route the first samples through your ordinary terminal. Do not request approval from Corporate Operations.”

> **CORPORATE OPERATIONS // SYSTEM NOTICE**  
> “No external client is registered under Route 09. All procurement requests must carry an authenticated contract signature.”

Player chooses **Inspect Request** or **Return to Factory**. This is narrative pacing, not a branching economic contract in v0.4.

### M8 completion

> **AUTOMATED ARCHIVE // MATERIALS VERIFIED**  
> “Copper and silicon match an erased engineering manifest. The original shipment records were signed by PROJECT PROMETHEUS. The attached circuit specification is incomplete.”

### M9 completion

> **UNREGISTERED CLIENT // SIGNAL RECOVERED**  
> “The first circuit works. Now keep the signal stable while the foundry continues production. We need proof the facility can power itself without depending on Headquarters.”

### M10 completion and cliffhanger

> **PROMETHEUS ARCHIVE // PARTIAL ACCESS GRANTED**  
> “Source of transmission: SECTOR 9 — SUBLEVEL 03. Sender registered 312 days after facility shutdown. Physical access: SEALED.”

> **CORPORATE OPERATIONS // PRIORITY OVERRIDE**  
> “Unexpected encrypted activity detected. Suspend investigation and transmit your latest circuit designs for inspection.”

The player retains free-play access, diagnostics and completed factory. The choice of obeying or defying Corporate Operations is **teased**, not implemented as a consequential branch; reserve it for a later campaign release. All story screens must be skippable/replayable, with no new mandatory modal interruptions during production.

## 8. Progression, compatibility and recovery

- New campaign: existing v0.1–v0.3 objectives remain unchanged; Chapter II follows successful M7 completion.
- Existing **v0.3 DONE** save: preserve the full layout, credits, research, diagnostic histories and proof that M1–M7 were completed. Present an explicit **Begin Chapter II** action rather than silently overwriting the prior DONE state. Activating Chapter II starts M8 from that exact factory without reset.
- Existing **v0.2/v0.3 active M6/M7** saves: preserve their original per-campaign `missionRuleVersion` (legacy v2 or v3) until M7 is completed. Do not retroactively apply v0.4 gates to old missions. New M8–M10 use a distinct chapter/mission-rule version (v4) so both semantics can coexist safely.
- Introduce versioned save migration for new item ledger keys, deposits, technologies, power state, mission progression, and recorder fields; do not invent previous sales, remove existing inventory, relocate structures or reset streaks. A failed migration must preserve the original save and present an explicit error.
- A previously completed foundry is a durable achievement even when the active campaign mission changes to M8. Do not infer historical Chapter I completion merely from a generic `DONE` token without checking migrated save ancestry.
- Starting Chapter II cannot require an unrecoverable purchase: test incremental construction and capital acquisition from an actual v0.3 DONE save, including a representative mistaken build/demolition sequence. Keep a profitable route available, and document recovery guidance.

## 11. Approved design direction and outstanding numerical/technical decisions

1. **Scope:** accept the recommended bounded site-wide power model for electronic fabricators only, with power networks/wires deferred, or explicitly rescope the roadmap.
2. **Physical layout:** select copper/silicon deposit coordinates only after testing migration-safe 12×12 configurations and real post-v0.3 layouts.
3. **Numerical economy:** decide new costs, revenues, rates, research fees, generator fuel-duration and supply/demand values from actual TypeScript-engine evidence, not isolated arithmetic.
4. **Mission difficulty:** accept M8 10+10, M9 4 circuits, M10 11 circuits + 5 steel + 57/60 powered ticks for a 60-tick qualifying streak as **hypotheses**, adjusting only after feasibility and human testing.
5. **Chapter initiation:** make **Begin Chapter II** an explicit action on old DONE saves, keeping prior achievement and free-play status visible.
6. **Story:** approve the anonymous client, Prometheus sublevel signal, and Corporate priority override as the Chapter II arc without implementing narrative branching yet.

## 12. Exclusions

Do **not** implement: offline progression, networked services, multiplayer, telemetry, new maps, storage, splitters, full electric grid/wires, wire and wafer processing, randomized outages, generic contract systems, AI/power research trees beyond this minimal chapter, or automatic retuning of earlier economic values.
