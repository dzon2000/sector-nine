# SEKTOR 9 v0.2 — WP5 Narrative, Tutorial and UI Acceptance

**Status:** Implemented; browser regression CI pending final green run.
**Branch:** `feature/v0.2-advanced-metallurgy`
**Specification:** `docs/specifications/v0.2.md` §§2, 5, 7, 8.
**Economic constants:** Unchanged.

## Delivered

### Missions
- Completion of M4 transitions to **M5 — Below the Surface**; existing M1–M4 progression logic is retained.
- **M5:** exactly ten coal delivered through the physical terminal after M5 begins.
- **M6 — Mechanical Precision:** eight gear deliveries after M6 begins.
- **M7 — The Old Foundry:** eight steel deliveries after M7 begins **and** permanent Advanced Smelting unlock.
- Mission-specific counters are independent of lifetime sale totals. Previously sold material qualifies for technology prerequisites but cannot automatically complete newly activated missions.
- Completion leads to **DONE — Foundry Restored**, continuing sandbox play rather than beginning Chapter II.

### Story
- Three-part Prometheus Foundry Directive opens when M5 becomes active, including on migrated M4-complete v0.1 saves.
- Players can skip or dismiss the narrative; later they can replay it from Operations without modifying mission or economy state.
- Narrative seen state is persisted in schema v2. Save migration preserves factory state, balances, layout and progress; pre-WP5 development snapshots with missing narrative fields receive non-destructive defaults.

### Contextual tutorial and interface
- M5 hint identifies coal deposit coordinates and Coal Extraction technology.
- M6 hint explains iron-ingot assembly and separation of technology capital from operating expenses.
- M7 hint explains two distinct inputs and blocked outputs.
- Construction palette includes Coal Mine, Mechanical Assembler and Advanced Furnace with prices and visible lock information.
- Iron and coal deposit coordinates are separately listed; coal deposit tiles are also rendered.
- Inspector exposes recipe, remaining cycle, typed input buffers, reserved inputs, output and occupancy of coal deposits under existing structures.
- Existing keyboard, pause, reset and introduction controls remain in place.
