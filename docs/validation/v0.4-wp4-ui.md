## 2. Issue #5 — exact pointer orientation

- Pressing a rotatable existing entity while `Inspect` is selected captures the original stable ID, pointer ID and client-space origin.
- Moving at least **12 CSS pixels** shows a live direction preview based on dominant axis (E/W for greater horizontal displacement, N/S otherwise); release inside the board commits exact intended facing **once**, irrespective of previous facing.
- A stationary/short click selects; same-facing drags are true no-op commands and do **not** record a turn or rewrite saved game.
- Escape, `pointercancel`, lost capture, invalid/outside release and non-left clicks cancel; releasing over an adjacent tile must not select/rotate that other entity. Terminal, placement and demolition cannot become drag-rotate targets.
- The global `R` shortcut, explicit `Rotate (R)` tool and selected-machine Rotate button remain. Each successful change uses the existing opt-in recorder's single `turn` command.
- Pointer-to-tile mapping uses the board's **actual rendered bounding rectangle**, not logical 44px coordinates. Tests exercise N/E/S/W, no-op, 3px accidental movement, Escape/cancel, terminal, adjacent tile, 360px CSS-scaled map, running simulation, reload and recorder counts.

**Automated evidence:** `tests/e2e/v0.4-rotation.spec.ts`; existing `tests/e2e/v0.3-playtest.spec.ts` recorder and inspector tests remain in full suite.

## 3. Issue #7 — fixed viewport, independent scrolling

- The app shell is bounded to `100dvh`, with bounded header and fixed bottom mission console. The center map remains square and completely visible without document-level scrolling, including while technology/inspector text expands.
- Left construction/research and right inspector/diagnostics/playtest are separate scroll containers with `overflow-y:auto`, `min-height:0`, and scroll overshoot containment; their content does not shift the map or mission HUD.
- The canvas is absolutely positioned within its square board wrapper to prevent Pixi's native 528px canvas height overriding aspect-ratio layout.
- On constrained/narrow viewports, accessible `Construction panel` / `Inspector panel` toggles expose the side tools as overlays rather than unworkable three-column layout.
- The mission HUD retains its objectives and locally scrolls long narrative/metrics, without page scroll. Header, map and mission progress are separate always-present regions.
- Browser checks run at **1280×720**, **1366×768**, **1920×1080**, unusually short **1280×600**, and a **390×700** mobile fallback. The expanded test uses a real selected conveyor, expanded research and playtest inspector to assert independent scroll heights and direct scrolling at large viewport. Page-level horizontal/vertical overflow is prohibited.

**Automated evidence:** `tests/e2e/v0.4-viewport.spec.ts`, with scale-adjusted legacy persistence, foundry, diagnostic and recorder browser suites.

## 4. Chapter II accessibility (headless WP2 exposed in WP4)

- **Begin Chapter II** is a deliberate, persisted UI action shown only for eligible Chapter I `DONE` saves; merely loading an old DONE save never starts it.
- Resource Surveying unlocks copper/silicon extractors, Circuit Fabrication unlocks fabricator/splitter, and Industrial Power unlocks generator. Separate 80/120/100 Cr research nodes expose prerequisites/status/affordability and require explicit purchase confirmation; all placement buttons show lock status.
- Once Chapter II starts, the fixed copper `(8,2)` and silicon `(8,9)` deposits become visible. Construction uses the already-validated headless paid technology and physical placement commands.
- Chapter II HUD shows M8 post-activation copper/silicon counts, M9 circuit sales/required tech, and M10 simultaneous **11 sold + 11 physically produced circuits, 5 steel sold, 57 covered nonzero-demand ticks and 60 qualifying windows**. Power diagnostics show authoritative supply, demand, served, unpowered and burned coal; generator/splitter inspector shows fuel and next outlet.
- No M8–M10 story sequence was added: presentation and the approved Chapter II narrative are WP5 work. The previous Foundry directive remains unchanged.

**Automated evidence:** `tests/e2e/v0.4-chapter-controls.spec.ts` (explicit transition, purchase/reload, deposit placement/inspection); WP2 legacy and paid headless integration suites remain intact.
