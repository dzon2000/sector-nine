## Owner playtest — 2026-11-17

The owner described the missions as challenging and approved the candidate. They stopped during M13 because they estimated rebuilding the factory would take about another hour. This is an estimate of remaining effort. The observed session lasted 1:12:22 and recorded 4,018 active ticks.

| Independent route milestone | Result | Active ticks after acceptance |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| M11 — Recover the Archive | Completed | 143 |
| M12 — Choose the Recipient | Completed | 285 |
| M13 — Reconstruct Prometheus | Accepted and unfinished at export | At least 84, based on the final recorded factory command |

The recorder attributed 41:33 to planning, 26:37 to mission or stability waiting, 1:44 to credit waiting, 2:25 to paused time, and 0:02 to unclassified time. These figures cover the full session. The export includes 308 placements, 227 turn commands, 218 demolitions and six research purchases. It reports save schema 5 and mission rules 5 with an unset build commit, so it cannot establish the exact played build. The owner did not report manually exercising save restore or restart. CI on the release candidate covers deterministic restore and both full contract routes independently of this playtest.

Release operations require [separate authorization](v0.5-release-checklist.md).
