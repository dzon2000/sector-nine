# v0.2 Headless Economic Validation — WP3

**Status:** Automated headless measurements captured; final release acceptance and human playtest remain pending.
**Design authority:** `docs/specifications/v0.2.md`, ADR-0002. No approved economic constants were changed.
**Fixture:** `tests/integration/economy/scenarios.test.ts`.

## Method

Each scenario runs 600 warm-up ticks followed by a 240-tick measurement period. Revenue and expense totals are cumulative deltas across the entire measurement period, not rolling 60-tick UI values. Construction and technology payments remain outside operating expense.

The legacy iron-ingot reference additionally asserts a fixed 60-tick window: 30 ore mined, 15 ingots sold, 10,500 cents revenue, 4,650 cents operating expenses, **5,850 cents profit**.

These are **standalone reference-layout comparisons**, not a complete staged campaign budget. Steel requires prior coal and mechanical technology achievements; investment and research paid earlier may be reusable. Real player cash flow and recoverability require separate AC-22 progression tests.

## Technical quality gates

- Existing v0.1 exact economy regression remains in place.
- Gear and steel independent lines can coexist on the 12×12 map and feed the terminal.
- A new per-material ledger records produced, consumed, sold and explicitly demolished units.
- Recipe inputs remain physically reserved during processing. Ledger consumption occurs at successful output completion; demolition accounts for still-reserved materials.
- Material conservation test audits all five item types across standalone coal/gear/steel and combined layouts over 1,100 ticks.
- Snapshot/restored simulation determinism is asserted for all three new reference scenarios.
- Separate accounting checks confirm capital purchases are excluded from operating expense.

## Remaining scope

WP3 validation does **not** establish end-to-end viability for new players progressing M4→M7, including ordinary build mistakes. A complete progression test and human playtest are required before v0.2 acceptance. Displaying 116.625 Cr/min as a two-decimal UI figure requires rounding; golden assertions operate in integer cents over the measurement window.
