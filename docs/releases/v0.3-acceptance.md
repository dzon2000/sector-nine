## Acceptance boundary

Code-based acceptance and playability are different decisions. Automated tests demonstrate physically attainable throughput thresholds, reproducible cash flow, migration and non-regression. They cannot establish how much human time is spent deciding vs passively waiting, whether players understand diagnostics, or whether M6/M7 require interesting decisions in practice. **Product-owner human acceptance must be recorded separately.**

Acceptance criteria V03-AC-01–17 and the technical parts of V03-AC-19–20 have test evidence listed in the integration matrix. ## Manual playtest A — fresh v0.3 campaign

**Starting point:** fresh browser profile or a deliberately reset game. Confirm **300.00 Cr**, new mission rules, and no pre-existing player progress. Do not change simulation constants or edit save data.

1. Start the game, read or skip the optional introduction, and open **Inspector → Local playtest → Start recording**.
2. Choose **Planning / routing**, **Waiting for credits** or **Unclassified** whenever relevant. These categories represent player-declared intent. They must not be inferred from pointer inactivity.
3. Complete M1–M4 and M5 (10 physical coal sales), recording whether tutorial milestones and Prometheus Foundry Directive can be skipped/replayed.
4. Enter M6. Observe that a single gear chain is insufficient; inspect input starvation, occupied output or transport restrictions. **Deliberately expand iron/ingot production**, reroute output if needed, and attain **at least 9 gears per 60 active ticks for 120 qualifying ticks**. Note the actual change that raises capacity.
5. Enter M7. Keep gears running while adding steel (2 ingots + 1 coal per steel) with Advanced Smelting. Demonstrate **simultaneously at least 7 gear sales, 7 steel sales and 175.00 Cr operating profit per 60 active ticks for 120 qualifying ticks**. Record physical rerouting and bottleneck inspection.
6. Pause during an active mission and reload the page. Verify there is no offline production, no loss of window/streak, and playtest time does not include the absent interval.
7. After DONE, stop and export **sector-nine-playtest.json**. Leave the report local until ready to share. Record observations separately from the export.

### Review interpretation and nonblocking UX candidate

The reviewer confirmed internal JSON consistency (time buckets add to wall time; 136 command events match counters; timestamps and mission transitions monotonic) but did not supply raw user command events for this repository. **No screenshots, subjective reactions, or individual throughput-window readings are asserted here.** The 61.5% unclassified segment prevents inferring that all remaining playtime involved active problem-solving. Action frequency indicates potential friction, not a proven usability defect. No gameplay/control redesign is authorized in this v0.3 review.
