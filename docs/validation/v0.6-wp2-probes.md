## Candidate measurements

A complete 30-tick window begins after activation. M14 requires six circuits produced and six circuits sold, with at most five consecutive simulation ticks without a circuit sale inside the window, including both edges. M15 repeats the circuit measurements and requires five gears produced and five gears sold in the same window. The observations use physical production and terminal-sale deltas through the existing contract summarizer.

The shared copper feed experiment replaces one conveyor at (7,1) with a north-facing splitter. Its forward outlet feeds the existing ordinary sales trunk at (7,0) and its clockwise outlet feeds the northern circuit fabricator via (8,1). Each route spends 9.00 Cr, receives 3.75 Cr in resale and spends a net 5.25 Cr. Forward priority diverts 2–4 raw copper per sampled block. Switching to clockwise priority costs zero and returns to a gap of five ticks with zero raw copper diverted in each of the final three sampled blocks. Payment, material consumption and terminal arbitration run through the existing engine. The terminal, main trunk and all structures outside (7,1) keep their IDs, locations and orientations.

The independent gear recovery experiment for each M13 route rotates the conveyor at (1,7) east toward vacant (2,7), then turns it north to restore the original path. Both turns cost zero. The terminal, main trunk and all structures outside (1,7) keep their IDs, locations and orientations. Fresh production and actual sales can still satisfy the initial candidate while the stalled line has buffered materials and gears in transit. A one-window gate will allow that transient success. The test observes the resulting deficit after the buffer drains.

For the raw-copper diversion, 120 modeled active seconds at 1× and 60 at 2× produce the same per-window traces and final engine state after 120 physical ticks. Increasing rate leaves both routes at 0/91 qualifying windows in this observation. WP1 covers the real scheduler separately.

## Verification

Run `npm test -- tests/integration/economy/v0.4-recovery.test.ts -t 'V06 WP2'` for the paid traces. Each `V06-WP2 WINDOW` line includes activation tick, elapsed ticks, qualification counts, first qualifying windows and all non-overlapping measurement blocks. `V06-WP2 EDIT` lines include local cost and layout edits.

Run `npm test -- tests/integration/economy/v0.6-priority-prototype.test.ts` for the headless splitter selector. The unchanged-engine stub initially failed all four orientation cases. The prototype passes seven cases covering both outlets across four orientations, fallback and blocked cargo, alternating default, pause and contention. The test command and TypeScript check passed locally. Human playtest remains pending.

Both prepared M13 endpoints satisfy both candidate gates as soon as each full window is available. This supports a low-friction chapter for prepared players and a measurable recovery on disrupted factories. This replay covers one representative factory per route. It leaves a compulsory puzzle for prepared endpoints unproven.
