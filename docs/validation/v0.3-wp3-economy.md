## Stockpile and invariants

- A disconnected single-source gear output line was allowed to backpressure and fill; reconnecting the terminal did not yield a 120-tick M6 qualifying streak.
- Prefilled assembler input buffers and reserved recipe inputs did not bypass M6.
- Prefilled assembler output, outgoing transport belts and typed input buffers did not bypass M6.
- Gear-only or steel-only factories did not satisfy the concurrent M7 objectives, regardless of profitability.
- Material ledger balances remained equal to physical on-hand stock after each test. Repeated JSON save/restore of F-M7 produced tick-identical future states, including arbitration and buffered materials.
- Paused/no-op ticks, mission-specific rolling windows, profit arithmetic and legacy missions remain covered by WP1/WP2 regressions.

## Full new-rules campaign from 300.00 Cr

The v0.3 campaign test starts with `begin(createGame())` and follows **mission rule version 3**, using real `place`, `turn`, `remove`, `purchaseTechnology`, and `tick`/ `runTicks` operations. The test does not inject cash, force unlock flags, or change recipes/prices.

| Reached objective | Active tick | Cash (Cr) |
|---|---:|---:|
| M3 | 26 | 232.50 |
| M5 | 173 | 292.95 |
| M6 | 450 | 418.40 |
| M7 | 690 | 438.90 |
| DONE | 870 | 712.00 |

The campaign includes a mistaken conveyor placement and demolition refund. It retains an earning route while financing the dual-fed assembler; then redirects the secondary iron feed to advanced steel production without removing the primary profitable gear line. Both reconfiguration phases keep the campaign recoverable.

Recorded financial ledger (cents): start balance 30,000; capital spent 73,500; refunds 2,625; research fees 38,000; terminal revenue 269,000; operating costs 118,925; final balance 71,200. The conservation identity is checked, not estimated. Total technology fees represent Coal Extraction + Mechanical Components + Advanced Smelting.

**Interpretation:** 870 ticks is simulation time, not real player session duration or a human usability result. The scenario demonstrates one constructible, recoverable campaign, not absence of every conceivable economic softlock.
