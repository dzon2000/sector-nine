## Approved technology gates and fees

| Technology | Cost | Preconditions |
|---|---:|---|
| Coal Extraction | 80 Cr | Complete M4 |
| Mechanical Components | 120 Cr | Complete M4 and lifetime sale of 20 iron ingots |
| Advanced Smelting | 180 Cr | Own both earlier technologies; sell 10 coal and 8 gears |

v0.1 `DONE` represents completed M4; later M5–M7 states also imply M4. Lifetime sale totals derive from terminal delivery events. The economic fixtures may preseed technology states for headless validation but UI purchases invoke the actual validated command.

## Acceptance criteria

- **V02-AC-10:** Prerequisites enforced, fee charged once, failures leave original state unchanged.
- **V02-AC-11:** Unlocks remain permanent after JSON save/load, demolition, and cash loss.
- **V02-AC-12:** Purchases alter cash only; rolling operating revenue, expense, profit and cumulative operating ledgers are unaffected.

## Compatibility / outstanding work

Migration maps historical v0.1 ingot sales into lifetime sales, so eligible players do not repeat tutorial milestones. WP5 remains responsible for narrative M5–M7, tutorial hints and full palette/inspector integration. WP6 remains responsible for full campaign end-to-end acceptance.
