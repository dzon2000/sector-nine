# SEKTOR 9 v0.4 — First Signal (Draft Release Notes)

**Draft only — not published.** Implementation lives on `feature/v0.4-first-signal`; release awaits product-owner approval, PR review and merge.

## Highlights

- **Chapter II — The Unknown Client:** new post-foundry M8–M10 missions, culminating in the Prometheus Sublevel 03 signal and Corporate Operations override. Messages are optional, replayable and do not interrupt production.
- **Electronics:** discover copper (8,2) and silicon (8,9), research their extraction, route both into Circuit Fabricators and sell actual circuits through the original terminal.
- **Industrial Power:** physical coal-fed generators supply deterministic site-wide capacity; fabricator processing stops when power is unavailable. Inspect fuel supply, demand and missing input/output bottlenecks.
- **Directional Splitter:** single-slot physical splitter with deterministic forward/clockwise output selection; no storage or item teleportation.
- **Factory UX:** one-gesture cardinal orientation of machines/belts, responsive centered 12×12 board, pinned mission objectives and independent scrolling panels.
- **Playtest reproducibility:** reports retain recording-start schema/rule versions, exact CI build commit when verified (otherwise null), manual mission-waiting versus credit-waiting buckets and observed power metrics.

## Balance and compatibility

The product owner explicitly approved Option A: copper rate 1 ore per 2 active ticks, 9.00-Cr Splitter, and new electronics/power constants and locations recorded in [v0.4 specification](../specifications/v0.4.md). M10 requires physical recent circuit production in addition to sold circuits, steel and covered demand; this guards against staged pre-activation stockpile shortcuts.

Existing M1–M7 progress, old iron/coal/ingot/gear/steel economy, deposits and terminal remain unchanged. Genuine old saves keep their rule version until Chapter II is explicitly started. No offline production or network telemetry is introduced.
