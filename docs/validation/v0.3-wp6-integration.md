| One steel source | 0 | 30 | 116.625 Cr |

A separate **full new-rules campaign** completed at **tick 870** from 300.00 Cr, with actual research expenditure and a recoverable misplacement, ending at **712.00 Cr**. The approved specification describes an earlier *exploratory* ~719-active-tick reference. The actual engine campaign requires **151 additional active ticks** than that exploratory result; the scenarios use different tested routes/capital sequencing and are not identical playthroughs. The exploratory result was not taken as a test expectation or an indication of human session duration. No changes were made to thresholds or prices to reconcile the difference.

Frozen economics: v0.1 ingots **58.50 Cr/60 ticks**, v0.2 coal **38.00**, gear **92.25**, steel **116.625 Cr/60 ticks**. v0.3 keeps all values.

## Human acceptance and release blockers

Only the product owner can provide credible evidence that the mission design makes deliberate factory optimization necessary and interesting in real play. Two playtests are required: (1) fresh new-rule campaign, (2) a genuine migrated v0.2 in-progress M6/M7 save. Record observed behavior with the optional local JSON recorder and short qualitative notes as outlined in [v0.3 acceptance](../releases/v0.3-acceptance.md).

Do **not** mark AC-18 or the human-observation part of AC-19 complete, close design issue #3, merge this PR, or create a release tag before the user verifies these results and authorizes release.
