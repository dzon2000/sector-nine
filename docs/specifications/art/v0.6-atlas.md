# v0.6 sprite review

- [Desktop board, 528 × 528](v0.6-board-528.png) uses 44 px logical tiles. [Scaled board, 360 × 360](v0.6-board-360.png) approximates 30 px tiles.
- These fixed review boards show every structure and item, exposed and occupied deposits, queued cargo, machine output, selection, and a rotation preview. The gear branch is blocked at the circuit fabricator. Coral cargo borders show blockage. Gold arrows identify the selected priority outlet. White outlines mark selection, cyan marks the rotation preview.
- Splitters in the fixture face N at (6,7), E at (6,5), S at (8,4), and W at (5,8). Their outlet pairs are N/E, E/S, S/W, and W/N. N is alternating, E and W prioritize clockwise, and S prioritizes forward.
- Small cargo and processor buffers need live review at scaled widths. Keep the inspector and accessible labels available. The artwork preview uses mock occupancy and status. Simulation and interaction still require in-game validation at both speeds.

## Atlas contract

[Frame metadata](../../../src/rendering/assets/sector-nine-v06.json) and [RGBA texture](../../../src/rendering/assets/sector-nine-v06.png) use PixiJS spritesheet format. Frame keys match the domain's 12 structure kinds and eight cargo items. Frames are trimmed, centered in a 44 × 44 source box with a 0.5/0.5 anchor, and packed with transparent gutters. Use 1× nearest-neighbor sampling. Place cargo in the upper-left independently of the structure and source it from existing belt occupancy or machine output.

The source conveyor and splitter textures face north. Rotate the splitter body around the tile center. The splitter body has outlets north and east at zero rotation. Other machine artwork stays upright so distinctive parts remain recognizable. Draw arrows independently from entity or rotation-preview orientation. For splitters, calculate the second outlet with the existing clockwise rotation rule and highlight the currently selected priority outlet. Keep arrows independent of occupancy and speed. Sell terminal has no output arrow.

The live board generates 44 × 44 conveyor sprites from adjacent belt ports. Matching end pixels join straight tracks, rounded corners and multiple feeds. Disconnected ports preserve a visible gap. The splitter keeps its atlas body and uses the same edge colors where it joins another belt. Rotation previews update all affected joins. The fixed atlas review boards above show the original artwork.
