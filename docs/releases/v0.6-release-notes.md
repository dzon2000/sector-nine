# SEKTOR 9 v0.6 — The Return Signal

- Switch between 1× and 2× simulation speed from the first tutorial mission.
- Follow Chapter IV after Project Prometheus. Prove circuit continuity in M14. In M15, ship three raw copper ore for repairs and restore circuit service.
- Choose forward, clockwise or alternating splitter routing in Chapter IV.
- Trace connected conveyor runs through rounded bends, merges and splitter joins. Cargo and direction arrows remain visible.
- See the mission identifier in the Operations Console through M15.

## Saves

Saves made before starting Chapter IV can continue. Earlier development previews that entered Chapter IV require a new campaign or a [fresh paid M13 checkpoint](../validation/v0.6-post-m13-launcher.md).
