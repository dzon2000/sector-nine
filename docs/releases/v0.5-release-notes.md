# SEKTOR 9 v0.5 — Draft release notes

## Chapter III — Project Prometheus

- Enter Chapter III after M10. Recover the archive, choose Independent or Corporate for M12, then reconstruct the shared interlock in M13.
- Preview contract requirements and track physical production, sales and qualifying windows in the mission console. Replay unlocked dispatches and keep operating after the final contract.
- The opt-in local playtest report adds contract transitions and route identity alongside its build and rule provenance and existing timing categories.

## Saves

v0.5 uses one save format from Mission 1 through all three chapters and free play. Current saves resume from the stored simulation tick.

Earlier saves require a fresh campaign. If the game finds an unsupported or corrupt save, it stops at a save error screen. Choose **Start new game** and confirm **Replace save and restart** to replace the stored save. **Cancel** keeps the stored data intact. Previously recorded playtest reports retain their original schema, mission rules and build provenance.
