# SEKTOR 9 — Version 0.1 Specification

**Status:** draft for implementation, subject to pre-implementation validation  
**Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Authority:** this specification wins over the GDD for v0.1 implementation details  
**Dependencies:** [GDD](../GDD.md), [Economy](../economy.md), [Tutorial and Story](../tutorial-and-story.md), [Validation Plan](../validation-plan.md)

## 1. Goal and scope

Ship a browser-based vertical slice in which a player:
- encounters the story opening and learns controls through a contextual tutorial;
- builds and operates a physical chain from a mine through belts and a furnace to a sales terminal;
- observes cash, costs, revenue, and net operating profit;
- completes four sequential prologue milestones;
- reloads the game without lost or duplicated progress; and
- continues in unrestricted sandbox after finishing the prologue.

Out of scope: research, coal, power networks, multi-tile machines, dynamic demand, storage, random maps, multiplayer, backend, cloud saves, offline production, mobile-first support, repeatable contracts.

## 2. Initial map and game state

- Rectangular grid: **12 x 12**; coordinates `(0,0)` at upper-left through `(11,11)`.
- Permanent inexhaustible iron deposits: **(3,5), (2,2), (2,9)**.
- Permanent, non-removable sales terminal at **(10,5)**.
- All placed machines and conveyors occupy a single tile.
- Deposits do not separately block placement of mines on their own tiles.
- Starting balance: **300.00 Cr**; no purchased objects or inventory.
- Simulation starts paused until the introductory tutorial begins.
- Directions: N/E/S/W; rotate clockwise by 90 degrees.
- Invalid placement is rejected with an explanatory message and no charge.

## 3. Objects, costs, and recipes

| Object | Construction | Function | Buffers | Operating cost |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| Iron mine | 45.00 Cr | 1 iron ore every 2 active ticks | 1-item output | 0.60 Cr per produced ore |
| Furnace | 80.00 Cr | Consume 2 ore to produce 1 ingot after 4 ticks | 4 ore input, 1 ingot output | 1.60 Cr per ingot |
| Conveyor | 5.00 Cr | Move 1 item forward 1 tile per tick | 1 item | 0.05 Cr per item accepted into a belt tile |
| Sales terminal | Prebuilt | Accept and sell at most 1 item per tick | No inventory | 0 |

Sales prices: iron ore **2.00 Cr** and iron ingot **7.00 Cr**, fixed during v0.1.

Construction is a capital expense. Demolition refunds **75%** of construction cost, rounded down to the cent. Destroying an object destroys the items in its buffers, after a confirmation warning; it does not destroy items on adjacent tiles.

Negative balance from operating expenses is permitted. The player cannot purchase while lacking funds, but existing machines keep running; a confirmed New Game / Factory Reset is always available.

## 4. Simulation contract

- **One fixed logical tick = one simulated second.** The logical tick rate is independent of render frame rate.
- Paused games perform no productive ticks. On hidden/inactive tab, pause without catch-up; v0.1 has no offline accrual.
- The mine has no inputs and only emits in its facing direction.
- The furnace accepts iron ore from neighboring producers/belts pointing toward it, except from its facing/output side.
- A furnace starts a four-tick cycle once two ore are available and its output slot can accept the future item.
- The furnace charges cost only upon successful ingot creation. A full output slot stops further completions.
- A belt receives items from any adjacent producer pointing toward that belt tile, enabling corners, but emits only in its own facing direction.
- The terminal accepts iron ore or ingots from adjacent producers pointing toward it, max one item per tick.
- At most **one tile of movement per item per tick**; no repeated moves inside a tick.
- Transfer resolution is atomic. Simultaneous departures/arrivals may fill a cell vacated in the same tick, but never overwrite a reserved destination.
- Competing inputs use deterministic fair round-robin ordering, preserved in save state.
- Blocked outputs propagate backpressure; no implicit loss or teleportation.
- No randomness in v0.1.

**Canonical tick phases:** finalize production -> plan and atomically commit transfers -> accept inputs/start cycles -> record financial events/update rolling statistics. A newly created item may enter its first belt in the completion tick but cannot move more than one tile that tick.

## 5. Economic benchmark

Reference route:
```text
mine E (3,5) -> belt E (4,5) -> furnace E (5,5)
             -> belts E (6,5),(7,5),(8,5),(9,5)
             -> terminal (10,5)
```

Capital: **150 Cr**. Steady-state per 60 ticks is expected to reach:
- **30 ore** mined and **15 ingots** sold;
- revenue **105.00 Cr**;
- extraction **18.00 Cr**, smelting **24.00 Cr**;
- raw-ore transport **1.50 Cr**, ingot transport **3.00 Cr**;
- target **58.50 Cr operating profit per minute**.

This is a pre-validation hypothesis. The warm-up period, timing boundary, and exact transport semantics must be tested with a headless engine before accepting this as an invariant.

## 6. Controls and interface

- Top HUD: cash balance, 60-tick revenue/min, operating cost/min, profit/min, run/pause.
- Left palette: mine, furnace, belt, selection and demolition, with prices.
- Center: 12x12 visible grid, deposits, objects, items, facing arrows, placement validity.
- Right inspector: entity status, buffer contents, recipe, direction, rotate/delete controls.
- Bottom: story/tutorial objective, progress, contextual hint, and feedback messages.

Inputs: palette selection then grid click places; `R` rotates preview or selected object; `Esc` cancels; clicking an entity inspects; `Delete` requests demolition; `Space` pauses/resumes when focus is not in a text field.

Visual states: `working`, `waiting_for_inputs`, `blocked_output`, `paused`. Use text/icons as well as color. Invalid commands explain the failure. No precise timing or reaction required for success.

## 7. First-launch story and guided tutorial

Show the corporate ultimatum and missing Prometheus archive *before* the first build interaction. Provide Begin/Skip and a way to replay the introduction. Narrative skipping must not prevent gameplay.

Four sequential prologue missions:

| ID | Mission | Completion | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Restore Extraction | Mine placed on deposit and one ore produced | Explain transport |
| M2 | First Delivery | Sell 10 iron ore | Explain processing |
| M3 | Added Value | Sell 20 iron ingots cumulatively in the save | Explain operating profit |
| M4 | Prove Profitability | Rolling 60-tick operating profit >= 50 Cr/min for 60 consecutive active ticks after window has filled | Prologue completed |

M4 resets its qualifying streak when profit falls below threshold. Sale-based missions count actual sale events (not produced inventory). Prologue completion unlocks *sandbox only*, not research mechanics. Milestones do not award credits in v0.1.

Tutorial should highlight valid tiles and belt facing, explain purchases and the distinction between money and profit. It must tolerate alternate valid layouts, permit experimentation, and never require developer tools or interaction once per tick.

## 8. Persistence

- IndexedDB, one local save slot.
- Save after topology changes and approximately every 10 active simulation seconds; attempt save on visibility change.
- Snapshot stores `schemaVersion`, map, entities and orientations, buffers, active production cycles, arbitration state, integer cash, last 60 accounting records, tick counter, intro/tutorial progress, and mission progress.
- Reload must reconstruct precisely the same logical state; no sales or production for elapsed wall-clock time.
- Invalid schema or corrupted data must raise an explicit recoverable error. Do not silently reset the save.

## 9. Modules and data model

The simulation must be independent of React, PixiJS, and browser globals. Suggested boundaries: `domain`, `simulation`, `game`, `rendering`, `ui`, `persistence`.

Illustrative, not final, data shape:

```ts
type Direction = "N" | "E" | "S" | "W";
type ItemType = "iron_ore" | "iron_ingot";
type MachineKind = "iron_mine" | "furnace" | "conveyor" | "sell_terminal";
type MoneyCents = number; // integer

interface GameState {
  schemaVersion: 1;
  width: 12;
  height: 12;
  tick: number;
  paused: boolean;
  balanceCents: MoneyCents;
  entities: Entity[];
  rollingEvents: { tick: number; revenueCents: number; costCents: number }[];
  mission: {
    activeId: "M1" | "M2" | "M3" | "M4" | "DONE";
    soldOre: number;
    soldIngot: number;
    profitStreakTicks: number;
  };
}
```

Represent processing, output-blocked, and idle cycles explicitly rather than assuming `remainingTicks` fully captures machine state. Recipes and prices belong in declarative definitions.

## 10. Acceptance criteria

| ID | Given / When / Then |
|---|---|
| AC-01 | A new game starts with 12x12 map, three fixed deposits, terminal at (10,5), and balance 300 Cr. |
| AC-02 | Invalid placement (wrong deposit, occupied tile, unaffordable purchase) is rejected, explained, and not charged. |
| AC-03 | Mine costs 45 Cr, furnace 80 Cr, belt 5 Cr; demolition refunds 75% without double-charging. |
| AC-04 | An unblocked mine creates one ore after two active ticks, charges 0.60 Cr once, and stops on pause. |
| AC-05 | Furnace consumes precisely two ore for an ingot after four active ticks, charges 1.60 Cr upon creation. |
| AC-06 | A correctly routed conveyor corner moves an item at most one tile per tick, without dropping it. |
| AC-07 | A blocked conveyor fills buffers up to their limits; upstream production stalls without lost items. |
| AC-08 | Competing suppliers cannot place two items in the same belt; arbitration is deterministic and fair. |
| AC-09 | Selling ore adds 2.00 Cr; selling ingot adds 7.00 Cr; counters increase exactly once. |
| AC-10 | Reference line reaches theoretical steady-state 30 ore/15 ingots and 58.50 Cr/min *after validated tick semantics*. |
| AC-11 | Rolling 60-tick operating profit excludes capital purchase/refund and is not shown prematurely. |
| AC-12 | M1–M4 complete in sequence; M4 requires full profit window plus 60 consecutive qualifying ticks. |
| AC-13 | Reload preserves layout, directions, buffers, progress, monetary events, and cycles; no offline catch-up. |
| AC-14 | Demolishing an occupied machine warns before destroying its buffer contents. |
| AC-15 | Operational debt prevents new purchases, not existing production; confirmed reset restores initial game. |
| AC-16 | Repeating commands and ticks yields identical results, with no unexplained item/money duplication or loss. |
| AC-17 | First-time player can complete tutorial/prologue without developer tools or per-tick manual clicking. |
| AC-18 | On first launch, an English story introduction precedes building; it can be skipped and replayed. |
| AC-19 | Contextual tutorial demonstrates placement, rotation, transport, processing, sale, and profit KPI. |

## 11. Non-functional criteria and work packages

- Deterministic test runs can execute thousands of ticks without real-time waiting.
- UI target: approximately 60 FPS on ordinary desktop at 12x12; frame drops never alter results.
- TypeScript `strict`; unit/integration tests for simulation and economy.
- Playwright end-to-end tests for onboarding and save/restore.
- No critical console errors or unrecoverable save corruption.

**Implementation order:**
1. Repository/tooling bootstrap and domain definitions.
2. Headless engine for tick, production, transport, and invariants.
3. Economic ledger and reference benchmark.
4. PixiJS board + React controls.
5. Opening story and tutorial/M1–M4.
6. IndexedDB save/reload and e2e tests.
7. Playtest balance and acceptance review.

A release is **not accepted** until AC-01 through AC-19 have been verified. Initial economics and pacing remain subject to evidence-based revision.
