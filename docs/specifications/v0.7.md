# SEKTOR 9 v0.7 — The Isolation Log

**Status:** Product Owner approved release direction and work-package plan on 22 November 2026. WP0 visual evidence and WP4 numeric mission rules retain their review gates.  
**Baseline:** Released v0.6 and `develop` at `562c837d3480c2984a26c1181727abe6546ab9a2` · [release](https://github.com/dzon2000/sector-nine/releases/tag/v0.6) · [merged PR #12](https://github.com/dzon2000/sector-nine/pull/12)

## Release goal

Make the factory easier to read and operate across the whole campaign. Let returning playtesters pick up at the start of a later chapter with a representative factory, then use Chapter V to test whether the revised console makes a real routing problem easier to understand and solve.

## Chapter V — The Isolation Log

**Premise.** The station responds after M15. Now that it can see Sector 9's circuit line again, it can read one side of the original isolation record. The other side sits behind a seized mechanical bypass. The station asks Sector 9 for gear shipments while it keeps the diagnostic channel open. The console reads like the same system the player uses to diagnose their factory.

| Mission | Factory decision | Story beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M16 — Open the Bypass** | Send a small, fresh batch of gears through the existing terminal while keeping circuit service available. The Operations Console shows both requirements together. | Station reads the first intact entry and discovers the interlock was triggered by an operator. |
| **M17 — Hold the Channel** | Sustain circuit output and gear deliveries together over multiple complete measurement periods. A combined progress view identifies which flow breaks first. A local branch or merge adjustment should be enough on both reference factories. An already capable layout can qualify. | Station retrieves the missing destination from the log. The operator isolated a downstream line to keep the station alive. That line is still sealed and gives the next chapter a concrete lead. |

**Draft transmissions**

> **STATION MAINTENANCE // ISOLATION LOG**  
> Sector 9, your signal is steady. We found the old isolation log. Its mechanical bypass is seized. Send gears and keep your circuit line running while we open it.

> **PROMETHEUS // OPERATOR ENTRY RECOVERED**  
> I isolated the downstream line. The station still had power. If the line comes back, check what was left on the other side before you open it.

Independent route receives the original voice transmission. Corporate route receives the same actionable request as a service ticket and recovers the same operator entry. Preserve the route in the rendering and archive. Use existing production, terminal and priority routing. Chapter completion adds the log to the archive once, then returns the player to free play.

**Feasibility before mission freeze.** Probe paid post-M15 factories from both routes and one human-like mixed layout. Seek a local solution that keeps the terminal and main trunk in place and needs at most one branch rework on each reference layout. Record actions, capital, cash floor, per-window output and timing. Demonstrate a balanced layout passes, a one-sided supply stalls, and local adjustment restores progress. Demonstrate progress at 1× and 2× from identical tick-indexed inputs. Choose thresholds and window semantics after the probes. If both reference layouts coast through both objectives, or meaningful solutions need a rebuild, revise M17 before approval. Prepared layouts remain valid without mandatory edits. Require fresh physical production and sales for both goods in M17. Use normal prices and costs throughout.

## Scope boundaries and decisions

- Keep the current grid dimensions, deposit/terminal positions, prices, costs, recipes, power, transport rules, and M1–M15 objectives. Any change to approved gameplay or economics requires an ADR and Product Owner approval. Campaign saves use the current-version policy in ADR-0006.
- Keep map expansion, robots, new production buildings, a new resource chain, and legacy save migration for a later release.
- Approved release direction includes the industrial console treatment, a playtest-only selector for Chapters II–V with paid starts, and the two-mission Chapter V arc. Exact typography and mission values are pending actual-size review and paid feasibility respectively.
