## 3. Successful constructed M10 fixture — not a mock

Three physical source streams and the steel terminal output share the original single sales terminal. Both fabricators receive real split ingredients. Two physically mined coal streams keep power supplied, while one coal stream also reaches the steel furnace.

The isolated throughput fixture starts with **1,000,000 cents** of *test-only capital* to decouple construction from cash; this is **not** recovery evidence. It warms up for 360 active ticks, then measures the next 300:

The complete 241-window streak is calculated from **real per-tick terminal sales plus real power demand/serving observations**, not from produced stock, preset mission completion, or a simulated timer. The full M8/M9/M10 progression is still not implemented, and the test does not claim that a mission object has completed.

### Cold-start mission timing (no injected materials)

The **same constructed physical factory** starts with empty material input/output inventories and was independently evaluated from active tick zero, without a 360-tick warm-up. Its first fully populated 60-tick sales/power window and 60 consecutive qualifying windows yield the proposed M10 completion criterion at **active tick 133**. No offline time, pre-activation stock or artificial power is counted. [CI #43578126966](https://github.com/dzon2000/sector-nine/actions/runs/43578126966) passed: 160 Vitest, 17 Playwright.

**Interpretation:** The 360-tick warm-up was only a steady-state benchmark protocol. M10 need not make players wait 360+ ticks from activation when the full factory is already built.

## 4. Failing controls and stockpile adversary

| Weakened physical fixture | 180-tick result | M10 outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Remove one circuit fabricator | 30 circuits, max 10 circuits/60 | FAIL: single-machine production ceiling |
| Remove one coal generator | 30 circuits, max 11 circuits/60, 0 fully-covered ticks/60 | FAIL: insufficient site-wide power |
| Remove advanced steel furnace | 45 circuits, zero steel | FAIL: simultaneous steel goal |
| Remove one generator's coal mine | 30 circuits, insufficient power coverage | FAIL: fuel shortage |
| Remove new-resource mines/fabricators, prefill 10 audited circuits on belts | 10 stockpiled circuits sold, zero active power demand | FAIL: zero-demand bypass blocked |

**Confirmed bypass of the provisional sales-only M10 gate:** A stronger physical scenario keeps **one legitimately powered and continuously operating circuit fabricator**, disconnects the other, stages **four pre-activation circuits** as audited opening inventory on physically disconnected conveyors, then rotates a conveyor at relative active tick 60 to release them to the unchanged terminal. The sale-window and nonzero-demand conditions then produce **63 consecutive qualifying windows**, above the required 60, even though one fabricator cannot sustain 11 newly manufactured circuits per 60 ticks. See [CI #43578492853](https://github.com/dzon2000/sector-nine/actions/runs/43578492853) and the adversarial fixture in `v0.4-option-a-m10.test.ts`. This is an **actual exploit in the proposed mission criteria**, not merely a speculative risk.

**Owner decision required before WP2:** Add **at least 11 circuits physically *produced* during each complete, mission-local 60-active-tick window**, *in addition to* the existing >=11 circuit terminal sales, >=5 steel sales and >=57 covered nonzero-demand ticks/60, for 60 consecutive qualifying windows. Production must be tracked as delta events from the authoritative ledger at each active tick after mission activation, not old lifetime stock. Keep M6/M7 and old economics unchanged. WP3 tests for the proposed guard are distinct from implementation and do not authorize WP2 to silently accept it.

**Adversarial proof scope:** The zero-demand stockpile control fails as expected; the combined-demand + four-staged-circuits control **passes** the old hypothetical gate and must **fail** under the proposed production-window safeguard. The full 12×12 two-fabricator layout must also continue passing the stricter guard.

## 7. Remaining acceptance gaps / stop conditions

1. **Critical: exploit reproducibly confirmed:** Four pre-activation circuits plus a legitimately powered under-capacity single fabricator create a 63-window M10 streak under sales-only semantics. Explicit owner approval of the proposed >=11 recent-production condition is needed; its positive/negative WP3 tests must pass before final gate.
2. **Owner authorization:** Splitter scope, 9.00 Cr prototype price, copper production change 1/2, both existing new deposits and unchanged M8–M10 thresholds are proposed values. No automatic approval from the green CI.
3. **Product experience:** Full rebuild uses 73 placements, which may create unreasonable manual routing time and UX burden; test a more incremental conversion or accept trade-off explicitly.
4. **Provenance:** Real-command v0.3 DONE test snapshot is deterministic but not an authentic player save; human save occupancy/retention and full Chapter II migration still require WP2/WP6.
5. **Research semantics:** Three projected research fees cannot serve as proof of actual WP2 research prerequisites or mission transitions; full campaign acceptance remains deferred.
6. **Code scope:** Splitter model and renderer kind mappings compile; there is no finished splitter tool/inspector/rotation UI, chapter progression, or v4 save schema yet.
7. **Conservative blocking:** Splitter only chooses immediately accepting downstream targets; it does not optimize simultaneous vacating chains. Could cause extra backpressure in other layouts and merits a dedicated routing UX playtest.
